# Instructions for Assistants and Contributors

- Prioritize reproducibility, traceability, and experimental rigor.
- Do not invent data, results, bibliographic references, or software versions.
- Clearly distinguish observations, analysis, and interpretation.
- Do not discard runs without documented criteria; preserve their records and
  explain any exclusions.
- Preserve original data and do not overwrite it with processed data.
- Document every design or protocol change and its rationale.
- Do not install, download, or perform destructive operations without explicit
  authorization.
- Do not implement the full experiment before technically validating the pilot
  and reviewing the design.
- Keep scripts simple, readable, and verifiable; do not generate fictitious
  experimental results.
- Briefly explain important technical decisions.
- Do not create commits or pushes automatically.
- Follow the MP-6104 specification and flag any contradictions rather than
  resolving them silently.
- Treat all eight conditions as candidates until their feasibility,
  architecture, and actual configuration have been documented.
